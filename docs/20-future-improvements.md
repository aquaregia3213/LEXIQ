# Future Improvements

## Advanced Contract Comparison

Compare two contracts and identify:

- changed clauses
- added clauses
- removed clauses
- modified obligations
- changed risks

## Contract Redlining

Potential future feature:

- suggest clause modifications
- highlight changes
- generate proposed alternatives

This must remain human-reviewed.

## Advanced Knowledge Graph

Add:

- temporal relationships
- organization hierarchies
- obligation dependencies
- cross-contract relationships

## Better Risk Models

Possible future approaches:

- fine-tuned classifiers
- legal-domain language models
- hybrid rules + classifiers
- calibrated confidence models

## Multilingual Contracts

Support contracts in multiple languages.

## OCR

Improve processing of scanned documents.

## Enterprise Features

Potential additions:

- SSO
- role-based access control
- organization management
- audit logs
- advanced permissions

## Compliance Monitoring

Monitor contracts against predefined compliance requirements.

## Contract Lifecycle Management

Track:

- renewal dates
- expiration
- obligations
- approvals
- responsible parties

## Agentic Workflows

Potential future workflows:

```text
Upload Contract
 ↓
Analyze
 ↓
Detect Risks
 ↓
Create Review Task
 ↓
Notify Legal Team
```

Agents should only be introduced when deterministic workflows become insufficient.

## Advanced Retrieval

Explore:

- rerankers
- hybrid lexical + vector search
- graph-enhanced retrieval
- query decomposition

## Model Improvements

Evaluate:

- local LLMs
- specialized legal models
- fine-tuning
- smaller task-specific models

## Scalability

If the system grows significantly, consider:

- background job queues
- distributed processing
- dedicated vector databases
- object storage
- service separation

Do not introduce these technologies prematurely.

## Long-Term Vision

The platform could evolve from a contract search tool into an AI-powered contract intelligence system covering:

- contract analysis
- risk detection
- compliance
- obligations
- contract lifecycle management
- relationship intelligence
- legal workflow automation
